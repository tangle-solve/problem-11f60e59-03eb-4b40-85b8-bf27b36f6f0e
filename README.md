### CHEM-000547: Efficient scalable photocatalytic water splitting with separated gases
- **Subfield:** Water splitting, hydrogen production and fuel cells
- **Open:** Combine efficient unconcentrated-sunlight use, separated hydrogen and oxygen streams and durable large-area operation with low mediator losses.
- **Status:** Pursued since the 1970s. A 2025 system achieved 2.47% solar-to-hydrogen efficiency and 1.21% outdoors in a 692.5 cm², week-long test.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** It would provide direct solar fuel while simplifying gas separation.
- **Key works:** H. Fu et al. (2025), *A scalable solar-driven photocatalytic system for separated H2 and O2 production from water*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-025-56314-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56314-x).
